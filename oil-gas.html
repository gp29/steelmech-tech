Steelmech Tech is an ISO 9001:2015 registered company that complies with API 8C, API 650, API 7G and CWB requirements. Steelmech Tech offers 100% in-house quality control inspections (UT, MPI and WPI) complete with support QA/QC documentation for all manufactured components and assemblies, coatings (liquid, powder, chrome, zinc phosphate, etc.) as well as cross border freight/logistics management.


For over 40 years Steelmech Tech has been actively involved in the manufacture and supply of over 300 different rig, top drive, HPU and down hole tooling related components, assemblies and projects relating to all areas of oil and gas production: drilling, research and development, processing equipment and facilities.


We guarantee confidentiality and non-disclosure. We develop modularization and fabricate key kits, providing our clients with turnkey assemblies to streamline their operations.


By continually communicating and working with our clients to better understand their needs and requirements, Steelmech Tech is able to provide innovative value-added services that achieve success for all stakeholders.

From customer supplied prints or concepts we can provide complete manufacture, QA/QC inspections, documentation, logistics for components and subassemblies such as, but not limited to, grabber assemblies, grabber legs, T-bars, bonnets, torque bushings, side plates, split blocks, subs, gearbox mounts, guards, hose reels, travel stands, catwalks, platforms, masts, crown sections, draw works, pump skids, engine skids, tank skids, transport skids, brackets and tanks.
