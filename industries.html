INDUSTRIES

With in-house engineering and state of the art manufacturing, SteelMech tech is a trusted and valued supplier of industrial equipment, manufactured components and heavy industrial equipment repair. It is our pleasure to provide custom designs, turnkey solutions, and repairable services to our clients in mining, pipe mills, steel mills and oil and gas, both within Western Canada and abroad. We look forward to growing together for many more years to come.

From manufacturing OEM components and assemblies to Level IV service rig recertification, SteelMech tech delivers custom manufactured services to all areas of the oil and gas industries production equipment.

Problems underground or on the surface? SteelMech tech in-house engineering team is committed to working with the client to provide a solution to your mining needs. Plus, we have full manufacturing capabilities to build or repair your mining products.
